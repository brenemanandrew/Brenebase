## Done
- New 3D mode "Picture to 3D in Blender (your PC)" (make3d/blender3d, backend blender-3d, runs on the PC).
- Routing: a pasted or attached picture plus "3D model" goes to it ("turn this picture into a 3D model", "image to 3d", "make a 3d model from this picture", "make this a 3d model" with a picture). Naming Meshy still goes to Meshy image to 3D. The planner prompt says the same, and a picture skill no longer moves these parts to Images.
- server.mjs: the pasted picture goes with the job (image64), plus name, detail (draft, normal, fine from words like quick or detailed) and colours (off for "shape only", "no colours"). No picture: fails with "Paste or attach a picture". Runs without an OK tap (free, only adds to the scene).
- PC worker: checks Blender answers on 127.0.0.1:8082, checks the graphics card is in 3D mode (gpu-mode.txt = 3d) and the 3D model maker answers on 127.0.0.1:8081, sends the job, follows it every 3 s with live progress, and reports "Added to the open Blender scene: <objects>". Plain errors when Blender or the 3D model maker is off. Counts as a graphics card job, so it never runs alongside pictures or the chat model.
- blender-3d added to PC_BACKENDS and the worker's abilities.

## Tested
- node test.mjs: 69/69 (62 before, plus 7 new routing and argument checks). The old case "Turn this photo of my dog into a 3d model" now goes to Blender, not Meshy.
- Full run in the cloud with the real server and worker, a mock Blender listener on 8082 and a stand-in on 8081: Chat with a pasted picture finished "Added to the open Blender scene: dog, dog.001"; the mock got the exact picture, name dog, draft, shape only. "make this a 3d model" with a picture also finished.
- Errors: no picture, graphics card not in 3D mode, Blender not answering, 3D model maker not answering. Each gave its plain message.
