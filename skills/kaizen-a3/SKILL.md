# Kaizen and A3 (work)

Private work. Everything stays on Andrew's PC model. Do not name people beyond their role unless Andrew does.

## A3 layout (one page)
1. Title and owner, date.
2. Background: why this matters now (safety, quality, delivery, cost, people).
3. Current condition: the process as it is, with numbers (units per hour, mispicks, dwell time, overtime hours) and where they came from (which SAP export or dashboard, which dates).
4. Goal: one measurable target with a date.
5. Analysis: 5 whys or a fishbone; separate facts from guesses; mark what was seen at the gemba.
6. Countermeasures: each tied to a root cause, with who and when.
7. Plan: steps and dates.
8. Follow-up: how it will be checked (metric, how often), and what happens if it does not hold.

## Other formats
- Kaizen event: scope, team roles, before numbers, daily plan, after numbers, sustain plan.
- 5S: area, the five steps with what was done and a photo checklist, the audit score.
- Standard work: steps in order, key points, reasons, takt or target time, the safety points.

## Rules
- Numbers over adjectives; say when a number is an estimate.
- Short lines Andrew can paste into a slide or a whiteboard.
- Never invent data. If the export is missing, say which one is needed.
